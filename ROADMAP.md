# Baraja Product Roadmap

## v1.0 - Generator (Current)

**Status:** Live on Mac App Store

Free AI-powered deck generator for Mexican Spanish.

**Features:**
- Single-topic generation
- Batch CSV generation
- Customizable difficulty and length
- 6 Anki themes
- Male/female voice selection

## v1.1 - Multi-Language (In Progress)

**Target:** 1-2 months

Add support for:
- Brazilian Portuguese
- Italian (Standard)
- Parisian French

## v2.0 - Designer (Planned)

**Target:** 3-6 months

Professional card designer with curriculum intelligence.

**Features:**
- Pattern dependency graphs
- Generation schema builder
- WYSIWYG card designer
- Custom curriculum sequences

**Pricing:** $29 one-time or $5/month

## v3.0 - Notebook (Planned)

**Target:** 6-9 months

Personal pattern library with voice capture.

**Features:**
- Voice/text pattern capture
- Built-in SRS player
- Semantic pattern search
- AI processing from real conversations

**Pricing:** $12/month subscription
